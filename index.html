What's inside

A digital handbook + blank templates + a clone-ready bundle. Seven modules:

  1. The Team Method — roles → deliverable contracts → artifact handoffs → codified standards. Why "division of labor + handoffs" beats "one giant prompt".
  2. Role Templates — three copy-and-fill templates: identity file, rules file, registry.
  3. Rules as Guardrails — turn your mistakes into hard constraints; the enforcement ladder from prose to tool-enforced.
  4. Registry & Naming — persona naming, registry upkeep, split signals, and the org design of going from 1 → N agents.
  5. Scaffolding Kit — why every file in a standard agent repo exists: bilingual README, VERSION + CHANGELOG, TODO pairs, tmp boundaries.
  6. Case Walkthroughs — two real repositories walked file by file: a 56-line minimal agent, and a discipline-maxed setup.
  7. Cross-Harness Portability — the portability map, five-step migration, one file on multiple harnesses.
